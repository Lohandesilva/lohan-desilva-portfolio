Churn takes a quarter of the customers and a third of the revenue

Operations · Healthcare

The most-quoted finding in this dataset is a confounding artefact

Patients who receive an SMS reminder miss more appointments — a result repeated across dozens of published analyses of this data. Reminders are only sent when the booking is far enough out. Hold lead time constant and the sign reverses.

How the work is put together

Every project ends in a recommendation, and every recommendation is anchored on a break-even rather than a point estimate. The point estimate depends on an assumption someone in the room will disagree with; the break-even tells them how wrong they would have to be for the decision to change.

Assumptions that are not observable in the data — contribution margin, cost of capital, loss given default, cost per unattended slot — live in one configuration file per project, with a stated basis, and are swept across a range. No conclusion in any of these projects rests on a single value for a number I could not measure.

Nothing is typed by hand. Each repository writes a metrics.json and every figure quoted in its README, executive summary and on this site is read from it. Running make analysis regenerates the whole report pack from the raw source file.

Where a result is dull or contradicts the expected story, it is reported as it is. Two of the five projects turn on a null finding.
