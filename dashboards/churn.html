Churn and retention economics

A 7,043-account telecom book: what churn costs, which accounts carry the loss, and where a retention programme should be pointed.

Case study Repository Executive summary

Churn takes 26.5% of the accounts but 30.5% of the revenue, because the customers who leave bill 21% more than the ones who stay. Two risk deciles hold 56% of the loss — which is what makes a targeted save offer viable.
