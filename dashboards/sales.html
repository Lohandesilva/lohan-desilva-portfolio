Sales performance and the cost of discounting

A four-year order book: where the margin goes, which lines destroy it, and what a discount cap is worth.

Case study Repository Executive summary

Revenue grew 51% over four years and margin ended lower than it was in 2017, because the growth was bought with discount. Past 20% off, a discount does not thin the margin on a line — it inverts it.
