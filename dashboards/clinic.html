Clinic capacity and non-attendance

110,521 outpatient appointments: how much capacity non-attendance takes, what actually predicts it, and what a service manager can do about it.

The most-quoted result in this dataset is a confounding artefact. Patients who get an SMS reminder appear to miss more appointments — but reminders are only sent when the booking is far enough out. Hold lead time constant and the sign reverses.

Lead time is the lever

Non-attendance against days between booking and appointment, with the 95% interval. Same-day bookings are missed 4.6% of the time; a month out it plateaus around 33%.

Source: 110,521 appointments, Vitória, Brazil, April–June 2016. Lead time from the date parts.

The SMS paradox, resolved

Non-attendance with and without a reminder, within each lead-time band. The same-day band has no SMS arm at all — that emptiness is the evidence.

Source: Mantel-Haenszel across 105 exact lead-day strata gives OR 0.769 (0.742–0.797).
